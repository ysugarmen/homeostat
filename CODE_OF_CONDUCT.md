# Code of Conduct

Homeostat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take criticism of work rather than people, and do not harass anyone.

It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at **sugaryoni3@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer will review each one and respond as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe: from a private warning up to a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
